Star Empire uses OpenAL Soft 1.25.2 for all sound and music.

soft_oal.dll is the unmodified 64-bit binary from the OpenAL Soft project, loaded
at run time, so it can be replaced with a compatible build of the library.

Project:     https://openal-soft.org/
Binaries:    https://openal-soft.org/openal-binaries/openal-soft-1.25.2-bin.zip
Source code: https://openal-soft.org/openal-releases/openal-soft-1.25.2.tar.bz2
